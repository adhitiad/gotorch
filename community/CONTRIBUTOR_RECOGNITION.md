# Contributor Recognition

GoTorch recognizes contributions beyond code: issue triage, documentation, tests, benchmarks, design review, accessibility, release engineering, and community support.

## Recognition principles
- Attribute work accurately and with contributor consent where needed.
- Prefer specific descriptions of impact over vanity rankings.
- Do not rank contributors solely by commit count.
- Invite contributors to opt out of public recognition.
- Maintainer status is based on trust and responsibility, not a reward tier.

## Ways to contribute
See CONTRIBUTING.md and the good-first-issue / help-wanted labels. A contribution is valuable when it improves correctness, usability, maintainability, or project sustainability.
