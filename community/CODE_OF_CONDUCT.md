# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
GoTorch aims to be a welcoming, professional, and constructive community. Participation is expected to be respectful regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, patient, and specific in technical disagreement.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome questions and explain context without condescension.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge others' work.
- Respect privacy and do not publish secrets or personal information.
- Follow maintainers' decisions and use the documented escalation path.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, intimidation, sustained personal attacks, sexualized abuse, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner or a maintainer. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially to the extent possible. Maintainers may issue a warning, remove content, restrict participation, or revoke project access depending on severity and context.

## Scope
This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project spaces, and when someone is representing GoTorch in public.
